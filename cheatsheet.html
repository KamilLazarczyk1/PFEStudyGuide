Electrostatic field

1 Charge properties
Quantization $Q=ne$; conservation; invariance.
2 Charge densities$$\tau=\tfrac{dQ}{dl},\ \ \sigma=\tfrac{dQ}{dS},\ \ \rho=\tfrac{dQ}{dV};\quad Q=\textstyle\int\tau\,dl$$
3 Coulomb's law$$\vec F=\tfrac{1}{4\pi\varepsilon_0}\tfrac{q_1 q_2}{|\vec r|^3}\,\vec r$$
4 E-field intensity$$\vec E=\tfrac{\vec F}{q_0}=\tfrac{1}{4\pi\varepsilon_0}\tfrac{q}{|\vec r|^3}\,\vec r$$
5 Work of E-forces$$W=q(V_a-V_b)=\textstyle\int_a^b q\vec E\cdot d\vec l$$
6 Voltage$$U_{ab}=V_a-V_b=\textstyle\int_a^b \vec E\cdot d\vec l$$
7 Potential$$V=\tfrac{1}{4\pi\varepsilon_0}\tfrac{q}{R}=-\textstyle\int_\infty^P \vec E\cdot d\vec l$$
8 Potential — superposition$$V=\tfrac{1}{4\pi\varepsilon_0}\textstyle\int\tfrac{\tau\,dl}{r}\ \ (\Sigma\tfrac{q_i}{r_i},\sigma dS,\rho dV)$$
9 Divergence$$\nabla\cdot\vec E=\tfrac{\partial E_x}{\partial x}+\tfrac{\partial E_y}{\partial y}+\tfrac{\partial E_z}{\partial z}$$
10 Poisson / Laplace$$\nabla^2V=-\tfrac{\rho}{\varepsilon};\qquad \nabla^2V=0$$
11 Gauss's law$$\textstyle\oint \vec E\cdot d\vec S=\tfrac{Q}{\varepsilon_0};\ \ \nabla\cdot\vec E=\tfrac{\rho}{\varepsilon_0}$$
12 Electric shielding
Shielded cable: conductor → insulation → shield → jacket; field confined.

Dielectrics

13 Polarisation types
Orientational; distortion.
14 Electric moment$$\vec p=q\vec d$$
15 Polarisation vector$$\vec P=\lim_{\Delta V\to0}\tfrac{\sum\vec p_i}{\Delta V}$$
16 Bound charge$$\sigma_b=\vec P\cdot\hat n,\quad \rho_b=-\nabla\cdot\vec P$$
17 Classification
Homogeneity, linearity, isotropy, dispersion.
18 Boundary cond. D$$D_{1n}-D_{2n}=\sigma_s$$
19 Capacitance$$C=\tfrac{\varepsilon A}{d},\ \ \tfrac{2\pi\varepsilon L}{\ln(b/a)},\ \ 4\pi\varepsilon\tfrac{ab}{b-a}$$
20 Poynting's hypothesis$$w=\tfrac12\vec E\cdot\vec D+\tfrac12\vec B\cdot\vec H$$
21 Dielectric examples
Vacuum 1, air ≈1, paper 3.7, glass 5–10, water 80.

Constant current

22 Electric current$$I=\tfrac{dQ}{dt};\quad \vec J=\sigma\vec E$$
23 Current densities$$\vec J=\tfrac{dI}{dS_\perp},\ \ \vec K=\tfrac{dI}{dl_\perp},\ \ I=\tfrac{dQ}{dt}$$
24 Current intensity$$I=\textstyle\int_S \vec J\cdot d\vec S$$
25 Continuity$$\nabla\cdot\vec J=-\tfrac{\partial\rho}{\partial t};\ \textstyle\oint \vec J\cdot d\vec S=-\tfrac{dQ}{dt}$$
26 Boundary cond. Jn$$J_{1n}=J_{2n}$$
27 Ohm's law$$\vec J=\sigma\vec E;\qquad U=IR$$
28 Resistivity of copper$$\rho_{Cu}\approx0.0175\,\tfrac{\Omega\,\text{mm}^2}{\text{m}}\approx1.7\times10^{-8}\,\Omega\text{m}$$
29 Resistance vs temp.$$R=R_0[1+\alpha(T-T_0)],\ \alpha_{Cu}\approx4\cdot10^{-3}$$
metals α > 0; electrolytes / semiconductors α < 0.
30 EMF$$\varepsilon=\tfrac{W}{q}=\textstyle\oint \vec E_{ext}\cdot d\vec l$$
31 Kirchhoff I (KCL)$$\textstyle\sum I=0\quad(\text{charge cons.})$$
32 Kirchhoff II (KVL)$$\textstyle\sum U=0\quad(\text{energy cons.})$$
33 Joule heating$$P=I^2R=UI;\ \ w=\vec J\cdot\vec E=\sigma E^2$$
34 Safety standards
Grounding + insulated gloves; $U\le50$ V (AC) / $120$ V (DC); $I\lesssim50$ mA (AC 25 mA).

Magnetic field

35 Grassmann's law$$d\vec F_{12}=\tfrac{\mu_0}{4\pi}\tfrac{I_1 I_2}{|\vec r_{12}|^3}[d\vec l_2\times(d\vec l_1\times\vec r_{12})]$$
36 Biot–Savart$$d\vec B=\tfrac{\mu_0}{4\pi}\tfrac{I\,d\vec l\times\hat r}{r^2}$$
37 Boundary cond. B$$B_{1n}=B_{2n}$$
38 Magnetic intensity H$$\vec H=\tfrac{\vec B}{\mu_0}-\vec M=\tfrac{\vec B}{\mu}$$
39 Ampère's law$$\textstyle\oint \vec H\cdot d\vec l=I_{enc};\ \nabla\times\vec H=\vec J$$
40 Maxwell–Faraday$$\varepsilon=-\tfrac{d\Phi_m}{dt};\ \nabla\times\vec E=-\tfrac{\partial\vec B}{\partial t}$$
41 Mutual induction$$\varepsilon_2=-M\tfrac{dI_1}{dt}$$
42 Self / mutual EMF$$\varepsilon_L=-L\tfrac{dI}{dt};\ \varepsilon_M=-M\tfrac{dI_2}{dt}$$
43 Magnetic energy$$W_m=\tfrac12 L I^2$$
44 Magnetic energy density$$w_m=\tfrac12\vec B\cdot\vec H=\tfrac{B^2}{2\mu}$$

Uniform plane wave

45 Maxwell (free space)$$\nabla\cdot\vec E=0,\ \nabla\cdot\vec H=0,$$$$\nabla\times\vec E=-\mu_0\tfrac{\partial\vec H}{\partial t},\ \nabla\times\vec H=\varepsilon_0\tfrac{\partial\vec E}{\partial t}$$
46 Helmholtz equation$$\nabla^2\vec E+\beta^2\vec E=0,\ \beta=\omega\sqrt{\mu\varepsilon}$$
47 Wave velocity$$c=\tfrac{1}{\sqrt{\mu_0\varepsilon_0}}\approx3\times10^8\ \text{m/s}$$
48 Plane wave
$\vec E\perp\vec H\perp\vec k$, transverse, in phase.
49 Wave impedance$$\eta_0=\sqrt{\tfrac{\mu_0}{\varepsilon_0}}\approx120\pi\approx377\ \Omega$$
50 Skin depth$$\delta=\sqrt{\tfrac{2}{\omega\mu\sigma}}$$
